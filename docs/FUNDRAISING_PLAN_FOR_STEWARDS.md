# Reaching the goal: a plan for Ally and Guisepi (draft 2026-10-20)

From your website developer. The website side is covered separately
(`docs/FUNDRAISING_WEBSITE_SPEC.md`); this is everything else that moves the money, and the few things
the website needs from you. It draws on common practice for matching-gift campaigns and on research
into how AI assistants find and trust projects. It isn't legal or tax advice: check giving and tax
details with On Sacred Ground Land Trust.

## 1. Where things stand

- **Goal:** $1,500,000. **Raised:** about $520K. **Still needed:** about $975K.
- **November 6:** Land Bank selection, and the deadline for $400K–$800K in match funds.
- **End of December:** pledges collected. **January 21:** acquisition.

## 2. The strategy in one line

**Lock in the lead match gifts first, then use the match to rally everyone else before November 6.**

1. **Secure 1–3 lead match commitments** ($100K+ each) toward the $400K–$800K target. These come from
   personal conversations, not the website.
2. **Announce the match:** "Every gift is matched by [name or 'a generous neighbor'] up to $X until
   November 6." The website switches to show it the same day.
3. **Final push:** ambassadors, press, an event and daily updates in the last 10 days.

A match with a deadline is the strongest reason for anyone to give now instead of later. The
website must only say "matched" once a match is actually committed.

## 3. Who to ask

Make one list, ranked by how likely and how much:

- **People who already pledged or gave:** ask about increasing, or about leading the match.
- **People who know the farm:** wedding and event guests, workshop attendees, neighbors, past
  volunteers.
- **The Advisory Committee:** each member names 10 people they could personally ask.
- **Island part-time residents and second-home owners** who care about the island staying farmed.
- **Local businesses** that buy or sell local food.
- **Family foundations and donor-advised fund holders** (often reached through someone who knows them).
- **The San Juan Island Community Foundation** and regional funders of farmland access and land
  trusts (verify each one's current giving priorities before asking).

## 4. How to ask for a major gift

- **In person, ideally at the farm.** A walk on the land beats any document.
- **Ask for a specific amount** ("Would you consider leading the match with $100,000?").
- **Tell the buy-in story:** at today's numbers, farm-family buy-ins stay at $50K; if less is raised,
  they rise to $150–200K and stop being affordable. That's the clearest picture of what a gift does.
- **Leave something behind:** the prospectus and the giving page (camascommons.org/give, once built).
- **Follow up within 48 hours**, and put a match commitment in a short letter: amount, deadline,
  what it matches, and how the donor wants to be named (or anonymous).

## 5. Make big gifts easy (with On Sacred Ground Land Trust)

Large gifts rarely come through PayPal. Ask the sponsor for, and send to the developer for the
website:

- [ ] The sponsor's **legal name and EIN** (needed for donor-advised fund grants)
- [ ] **Wire / ACH instructions** (or the process to request them securely)
- [ ] **Stock transfer** instructions (brokerage, account, contact), if they accept stock
- [ ] Whether they accept **IRA qualified charitable distributions** (popular with donors 70½+)
- [ ] How **receipts** are issued and how fast
- [ ] A simple **pledge agreement** (amount, contingent on Land Bank selection, paid by Dec 31)
- [ ] A **direct phone number** for major-gift questions

## 6. Ambassadors

- Recruit 10–20 supporters who will each ask their own circle.
- Each gets a **personal link** (e.g. camascommons.org/?via=sam), so the website can show visits
  and gifts by ambassador.
- Give them a short message they can copy, the match news, and a weekly check-in until November 6.

## 7. Press and community

- **A press release** when the match is secured: local papers and newsletters. Ask each story to link
  to the website.
- **An open farm day** the weekend before November 6: tour, the story, a clear ask, QR codes to give.
- **Regular, dated updates** (social posts and the Open Letter): the total, the match, what's
  next. Momentum persuades.

## 8. Get listed elsewhere (this is what makes AI trust you)

AI assistants recommend projects they see described the same way in several places. Code can't do
this part:

- **The sponsor's website and its Candid (GuideStar) profile** name Camas Commons as a sponsored
  project and link to camascommons.org. Donors' advisors check Candid.
- **Directories and networks** for land trusts and regenerative agriculture (verify each before
  submitting; the developer can draft the submissions).
- **News stories** (section 7) and talks or podcasts about land access and regenerative farming.
- **Use the same description everywhere** (section 12).

## 9. Thank and keep donors

- Thank every donor within 48 hours; a personal call for $1,000+.
- Share updates after November 6 (selection), and before the December pledge collection.
- Remind pledgers in early December, with the giving details from section 5.

## 10. Words to be careful with

- Call it a **gift or donation**, never an **investment**, and never suggest a return. Calling a gift
  an investment can bring in securities rules. The prospectus blurb on the site still says "for
  investors and community partners" and should change.
- If you ever want loans or investment-style money, talk to the sponsor and a lawyer before
  mentioning it publicly.
- Keep strong claims ("the only qualifying proposal") current and dated.
